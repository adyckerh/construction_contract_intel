                            PROJECT DESCRIPTION

PROJECT NAME: Riverside Mixed-Use Development - Phase II
PROJECT LOCATION: 2400 East Riverside Drive, Austin, Texas 78741
APN: 04-2847-0012-00

PROJECT SCOPE:
Construction of a 6-story mixed-use building containing:
- Ground floor retail space: 18,500 square feet
- Residential units (floors 2-6): 85 units total
- Underground parking structure: 142 spaces
- Rooftop amenity deck with pool
- Associated site improvements and landscaping

BUILDING SPECIFICATIONS:
- Total Building Area: 127,000 gross square feet
- Building Height: 78 feet
- Structural System: Post-tensioned concrete
- Exterior: Brick veneer with aluminum curtain wall system
- HVAC: Central plant with individual unit controls

- Building envelope (walls, roof, windows, doors)
- Interior partitions and finishes

- Shop drawings for all structural and architectural elements

- FF&E (Furniture, Fixtures & Equipment) for common areas

4.4 EXCLUSIONS
The following items are NOT included in this Contract:
- Tenant improvements beyond base building

================================================================================
                    ARTICLE 5: CHANGE ORDERS
================================================================================

5.1 CHANGE ORDER PROCESS

5.1.1 Owner-Initiated Changes
Owner may request changes to the Work at any time by written Change Order
Request (COR). Contractor shall respond with pricing within 10 business days.

5.1.2 Contractor-Initiated Changes
If Contractor identifies conditions requiring changes, Contractor shall submit
a Request for Information (RFI) or Change Order Proposal within 5 business days
of discovery.

5.1.3 Required Approvals
- Changes under $25,000: Project Manager approval
- Changes $25,000 - $100,000: Director of Development approval
- Changes over $100,000: Board of Directors approval (14-day review period)

5.2 CHANGE ORDER PRICING

5.2.1 Labor Rates (per hour, including burden):
- Superintendent: $95.00
- General Foreman: $85.00
- Journeyman: $72.00
- Apprentice: $48.00
- Laborer: $42.00

5.2.2 Markups:
- Contractor overhead on subcontractor work: 10%
- Contractor profit on subcontractor work: 5%
- Contractor overhead on self-performed work: 15%
- Contractor profit on self-performed work: 10%
- Bond premium adjustment: 1.5%

5.3 TIME EXTENSIONS
Change Orders affecting the critical path shall include appropriate time
extensions. Contractor shall provide CPM schedule analysis demonstrating
impact within 14 days of Change Order execution.

================================================================================
                    ARTICLE 9: DISPUTE RESOLUTION
================================================================================

9.1 NEGOTIATION
The parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through direct
negotiation within 15 business days.

9.2 MEDIATION
If negotiation fails, disputes shall be submitted to non-binding mediation
before a mutually agreed mediator. Costs shall be shared equally.

9.3 ARBITRATION
If mediation fails, disputes shall be resolved by binding arbitration in
accordance with the Construction Industry Arbitration Rules of the American
Arbitration Association. Arbitration shall take place in Austin, Texas.

9.4 CONTINUATION OF WORK
Pending resolution of disputes, Contractor shall continue performance of the
Work and Owner shall continue to make undisputed payments.

================================================================================
                    ARTICLE 10: TERMINATION
================================================================================

10.1 TERMINATION FOR CAUSE
Owner may terminate for cause if Contractor:
- Fails to supply adequate labor or materials
- Fails to make payments to subcontractors
- Persistently violates laws or contract requirements
- Substantially abandons the Work

Owner shall provide 7 days written notice and opportunity to cure.

10.2 TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE
Owner may terminate for convenience upon 14 days written notice. Contractor
shall be entitled to payment for:
- Work completed to date
- Reasonable demobilization costs
- Overhead and profit on work not performed (5% of remaining Contract Sum)

10.3 CONTRACTOR'S RIGHT TO TERMINATE
Contractor may terminate if Owner:
- Fails to make payment within 60 days of approved application
- Suspends work for more than 90 consecutive days
- Materially breaches contract obligations

================================================================================
                    ARTICLE 11: SAFETY AND COMPLIANCE
================================================================================

11.1 SAFETY PROGRAM
Contractor shall implement a comprehensive safety program complying with OSHA
regulations and Owner's site safety requirements. A full-time Safety Manager
shall be on-site during all construction activities.

11.2 REGULATORY COMPLIANCE
Contractor shall comply with all applicable:
- Building codes and standards
- Environmental regulations
- OSHA requirements
- ADA accessibility requirements
- Local ordinances and permits

11.3 SUSTAINABILITY REQUIREMENTS
The Project shall achieve LEED Silver certification (minimum). Contractor shall:
- Divert 75% of construction waste from landfill
- Use materials with recycled content as specified
- Implement construction IAQ management plan
- Document all LEED credits during construction

================================================================================
                    ARTICLE 12: SPECIAL CONDITIONS
================================================================================

12.1 NOISE RESTRICTIONS
Construction activities generating noise above 85 dB shall be limited to:
- Monday through Friday: 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- Saturday: 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- No work on Sundays or major holidays

12.2 TRAFFIC MANAGEMENT
Contractor shall maintain access to adjacent businesses at all times. Heavy
deliveries shall be scheduled between 9:00 AM and 3:00 PM to avoid rush hours.

12.3 NEIGHBORHOOD COMMUNICATION
Contractor shall provide monthly construction updates to adjacent property
owners and maintain a 24-hour complaint hotline.

12.4 ARCHAEOLOGICAL DISCOVERY
If archaeological or paleontological materials are discovered, Contractor shall
immediately cease work in that area and notify Owner. Work may not resume until
authorized by the Texas Historical Commission.
